German Language Levels (CEFR)

Easy explanations for students in grades 5–10 and their parents. Pick a level below to see exams, official links and what they mean in Austria.

A1

Understands and uses simple everyday phrases and can introduce themselves.
A2

Can talk about familiar topics like family, shopping and their surroundings.
B1

Can handle most everyday situations and describe experiences and plans.
B2

Can discuss a wide range of topics clearly, including technical subjects.
C1

Can use German flexibly for social, academic and professional purposes.
C2

Can understand and express almost anything with precision and nuance.
