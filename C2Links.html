German Language Levels (CEFR)

Easy explanation for students in grades 5–10 and their parents.

See Full CEFR Table

Read the six overall descriptions together.

LevelIn plain words
A1 Understands and uses simple everyday phrases and can introduce themselves.
A2 Can talk about familiar topics like family, shopping and their surroundings.
B1 Can handle most everyday situations and describe experiences and plans.
B2 Can discuss a wide range of topics clearly, including technical subjects.
C1 Can use German flexibly for social, academic and professional purposes.
C2 Can understand and express almost anything with precision and nuance.

This is a short summary only. For the full official global scale, see the Council of Europe CEFR table.
